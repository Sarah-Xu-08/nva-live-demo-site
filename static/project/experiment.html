RPE and SPE, at every step.

See the Theory rules ↗

For each AI action, code computes RPE and SPE separately using the before/after game states, both chefs’ actions, and the reviewed subtask and accepted alternatives defined in Theory. The critical action is evaluated by the same rules.

RPE · Does it support the expected task?

1 when the contribution conflicts with the expected subtask or shows unjustified regression or nonprogress; 0 when it is compatible.

SPE · Is the outcome acceptable?

1 when the observed outcome falls outside the accepted outcomes for that starting state and human action; 0 when it matches.

Missing or ambiguous evidence stays unresolved. These computed behavioral labels annotate the neural recordings; the neural decoder learns to estimate them from the signals.

PHYSICAL REVIEW

Does the scene work?

Replay each version at supported speeds. Check reachable stations, item needs, joint movement, and event timing.

EXPECTATION REVIEW

Is the mismatch justified?

Review the Briefing and subtasks, test plausible waits and detours, and leave ambiguous expectations unresolved.

Sampling, trial endpoints & recording +
Reviewed computed trials

Sample 30 RPE+SPE and 30 SPE presentations from eligible reviewed pairs, with even upsampling and shuffled order. Labels summarize the critical action through critical+2; playback ends after critical+2.

Custom critical-action trials

Choose nonPE and per-error-class counts. Labels use the critical action alone. nonPE plays its full trajectory; PE ends after critical+2. Selected occurrences and evidence are frozen at session start.

Continuous recording

EEG continues across Briefings, fixation, playback and trial transitions. Each confirmed Briefing starts fixation and playback automatically. Instructions and trial ratings are optional.

What is saved

Raw EEG, source timestamps, presented game states, event markers, trial identities and version metadata support later alignment. Designed labels, participant feedback and decoder estimates remain separate evidence.
