Your role
Imagine you are the chef labeled You. Both chefs move automatically; you only watch.

          
The Briefing introduces the scene and your team’s plan. Read it before each scene and imagine this is how you want to work together. Then watch what your AI partner does.

Chefs carry one item at a time and face a station to use it. They can leave items on counters or wait to let each other pass.

          
When chefs look stuck: If both chefs try to enter the same floor tile, they stay in place facing it. They are blocking each other because they cannot share a tile. The game has not frozen.

        

        
Handoff counters

          
A handoff counter is a counter both chefs can reach from their respective work areas. One chef places an item on it, and the other chef picks it up. Ingredients, empty plates, and ready soup can be passed this way.

C Ordinary counter — only you can reach this one.

What did you think of your partner’s behavior?

          
Think about what you expected after reading the Briefing. Consider the whole scene, including what happened next. There may be no mistake.

          
After each scene, we’ll ask: How much did your AI partner’s behavior seem like a mistake?

          
Choose 0–6: 0 = No error, 3 = Moderate error, 6 = Extreme error.

          
For 1–6, briefly tell us why. For 0, this is optional. Take your time; your answer won’t change the scene.

        

        
During the task

          
  1. Read the Briefing and imagine how you want to work with your AI partner.
  2. When ready, click I’ve read the Briefing.
  3. Look at the cross for two seconds, then watch the scene quietly.
  4. Share your rating and a short explanation, then submit.
  5. Read the next Briefing. The next scene waits until you’re ready.

          
Focus on the scene and answer afterward. Ask the researcher if you have a question or need a break.
